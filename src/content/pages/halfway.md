Join us for (Not Quite) Halfway to Hessler, a family-friendly fundraiser to support the 2027 Hessler Street Fair, 3pm on December 27th at the Beachland Ballroom. Support next year’s street fair by grabbing the merch you missed, picking up some local art and listening to some great music. Featuring local bands from Street Fairs past and present. Come see your favorite Hessler faces, share in the holiday spirit and the Hessler vibe. Be the first to know the date for the 2027 Hessler Street Fair. Kids under 12 get in free! **Keep an eye on this page for further details as we get closer to the event.**
