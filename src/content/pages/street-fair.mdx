The Hessler Neighborhood Association is pleased to announce the return of the Hessler Street Fair! This one-day event will occur on Saturday, October 3rd from noon until 7pm in the heart of University Circle. Featuring music, fine art, food, lemonade, knowledge and inspiration, the Fair will appeal to generations young and old. We look forward to seeing you on the street.
