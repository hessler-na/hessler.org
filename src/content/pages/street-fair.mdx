The Hessler Neighborhood Association is pleased to announce the return of the Hessler Street Fair! This one-day event will occur on Saturday, October 3rd from noon until 7pm in the heart of University Circle. Featuring music, fine art, food, lemonade, knowledge and inspiration, the Fair will appeal to generations young and old. We look forward to seeing you on the street.

## Band list
### 12 PM: Ms. BBC & Di Queendom
![Ms. BBC & Di Queendom](/images/msbbc.jpeg)
> Ms. BBC & Di Queendom is a musical and cultural project centered on feminine nurturing, empowerment, and reggae/African-influenced performance art. The group blends reggae, roots, and African dance elements, often performing with live accompaniment from groups like the Organik Sound Family and Dubbstarr.

### 1 PM: Austin Walkin’ Cane
![Austin Walkin' Cane](/images/austinwalkincane.jpeg)
> **Damn Fine Blues!** Original & classic delta blues featuring slide guitar and soulful vocals.  A blues gumbo repertoire that can conjure up the ghosts of highway 61 & the delta blues experience.  Austin Walkin’ Cane is a blues singer, songwriter and slide guitarist that performs acoustic solo, duo & electric band sets. Austin Walkin’ Cane has toured Australia, Nepal, Colombia, France, Germany, England, Wales, and the US, from New Orleans, Louisiana to Juneau, Alaska with only a guitar & suitcase in hand.

### 2 PM: Country Honk
![Country Honk](/images/countryhonk.jpeg)
> Known for their dynamic live shows and sharp songwriting, Country Honk draws inspiration from legends like Hank Williams, Buck Owens, Merle Haggard, and Willie Nelson, while channeling the spirit of the country rock movement of the ’60s and ’70s.

### 3 PM: Hoodoo BBQ
![Hoodoo BBQ](/images/hoodoo.jpg)
> Hoodoo BBQ’s sound is a powerful mix of roots rock, rockin’ blues, toe-tappin' rockabilly, classic country, straight-up rock-and-roll, and hard-driving originals. This broad, infectious blend captures a multigenerational audience that stays engaged through last call. Together, we deliver high-energy entertainment that turns first-time listeners into long-time fans.

### 4 PM: Charlie Mosbrook & Friends
![Charlie Mosbrook & Friends](/images/charliemosbrook.jpeg)
> In the tradition of Woody Guthrie, Charlie uses his music as a vehicle to build a better world through programs like Music to Life, Stop the Hate, monthly performances in the local spinal cord injury unit, and countless charities and events that advocate for human rights and environmental action. Charlie’s music is both informed and inspired by the amazing individuals who give themselves to these causes. He does not see his music as political, but honest. Honest about the events the songs are written about. Honest about the impact these events had on our lives, and honest about the agency and advocacy resulting from these events. Charlie believes that if you have an honest song, the truth will shine.

### 5 PM: The JiMiller Band
![The JiMiller Band](/images/jimiller.jpg)
> The JiMiller Band's style is as varied as their influences; JiMiller blends together blues, country, Cajun, rock 'n' roll, jazz and bluegrass into a musical Americana that is both fresh and timeless in their everlasting sets. However, the band is best known for its freeform improvisation rock that was made famous by The Grateful Dead and Allman Brothers. Jim Miller has been playing music for the past 50 years. Jim was the former frontman of the band Oroboros, orchestrating their 18-year odyssey on the road, opening for the 1994 H.O.R.D.E. Festival, the 1996 Further Festival, The Kinks, Hot Tuna, Santana, Richard Thompson, Robert Hunter and members of the Grateful Dead. He has also had the privilege of sharing the stage and jamming with Blues Traveler, Sheryl Crow, The Allman Brothers, Hot Tuna and the legendary John Cipollina of Quicksilver Messenger Service. In 1994 Jim opened for Phish at the Cleveland Agora.

### 6 PM: Hello!3D
![Hello!3D](/images/hello3d.jpeg)
> Hello! 3D is like a weird experiment that has gone right. A seven-piece from Cleveland, Ohio whose musical passport is filled with stamps from Peru, Colombia, and other corners of the psychedelic globe.
